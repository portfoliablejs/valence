# `ds-case-view`

The `ds-case-view` component is a **stateless (presentational)** Template that composes `ds-header` and `ds-article` into a single case-study detail surface for SPA shells.

---

## Principles

### Stateful Host, Stateless Template
The template follows **State In / Events Out**:
* **State In (Attributes / Properties):** The host app controls all breadcrumb, navigation, and article labels/visibility toggles.
* **Events Out (Pass-through):** `ds-breadcrumb-*`, `ds-navigation-menu-*`, `ds-article-action`, and `ds-article-share` events bubble to the host unchanged.
* **No Smart Routing Logic:** Route switching, deep-linking, data fetching, and analytics remain outside this template.

### Composition Boundary
`ds-case-view` provides structure, not content ownership:
* **Header Region:** A full-width `ds-header` aligned with HomeView behavior.
* **Article Region:** A centered `ds-article` with slot re-projection for thumbnail, summary, player, default body, and navigator.
* **Motion Compatibility:** Transition orchestration is intentionally host-managed so reduced-motion and route-history policies stay centralized.

## Accessibility & Semantics

* **Semantic Landmark:** Renders a top-level `<section>` with host-provided `aria-label` for case detail context.
* **Keyboard and Tooltip Semantics:** Delegated to composed children (`ds-header`, `ds-article`, and their molecules/atoms).
* **Reduced Motion Strategy:** The template does not impose route transitions internally, enabling host shells to centralize reduced-motion handling for all route changes.
